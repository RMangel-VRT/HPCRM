Full Feature Overview

Build an internal Service Checklist / Contract Execution View for each customer and property that gives the team a single operational place to see the full year of committed services, their service windows, and their current status. This feature should not create a new scheduling engine or separate completion workflow. It must sit on top of the existing campaigns system and use that system as the source of truth wherever possible. The new tool should aggregate all campaign-linked services tied to a given customer or property, organize them into a clean checklist or seasonal timeline, and help the team quickly understand what has been completed, what is currently in window, what is upcoming, and what may be missed or overdue. The purpose is to improve operational clarity for any given account without duplicating existing campaign logic.

Core Product Principle

This tool is a view layer and contract execution layer, not a replacement for campaigns.

Existing system remains responsible for:
creating campaigns
assigning customers
defining service windows
checking off completion
managing execution workflow
New checklist tool becomes responsible for:
customer-specific operational visibility
full-year checklist organization
seasonal grouping
service status rollups
quick answer to “where does this customer stand right now?”

That distinction is what keeps this build clean.

Slice 1 — Service Checklist Foundation (Read-Only Customer View)
What & Why

Build the first internal view that surfaces all campaign-linked services for a selected customer in one place. This is the foundational slice and should be read-only. The goal is to prove the concept by showing one customer’s campaign obligations as a usable checklist without introducing any new operational logic yet.
