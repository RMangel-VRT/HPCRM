# Slice 05 — Pulse rail, and retiring the stat cards

Frontend. **This slice deletes code** and finalizes the page layout — review the diff for removals.

**Assumes the ground rules doc (00-RULES.md).** The ones that bite hardest here:
**not everything is a card**; lime `--primary` is reserved; every colour from a token in both
themes; contracts have **no signed/unsigned state**.

Depends on slice 02 (`GET /api/dashboard/pulse`), and lands on top of slices 03 and 04.

---

## Goal

Build the right-hand column, and delete the four vanity stat cards that currently open the page.

Customer count, contract count, monthly revenue and YTD revenue are numbers you *read*, not things
you *do* — putting them first made the least actionable content on the page its most prominent.
They survive, demoted, as a hairline ledger in the rail.

## Rail sections, top to bottom

**1 — Revenue pace.**
YTD as one large figure with the delta vs the prior year beside it, then a small area chart of
`revenue.months` with `revenue.priorMonths` as a dashed grey reference line.

- **One y-scale.** Never two axes.
- Gridlines and axis text recessive; every axis label names a value the chart actually reaches.
- The current month's point gets an emphasized marker and a direct label.
- **Both series labelled** — identity is never colour alone.
- Chart text takes theme tokens so it stays legible in dark mode; every drawn shape gets an explicit
  fill; leave room in the viewBox for the outermost labels.
- The current-year series is the only thing on this page besides the primary button allowed to use
  lime `--primary`.

**2 — Ledger.** `label … value` pairs on 1px rules — **not stat cards, not tiles**:

- September billed
- Active customers
- Active contracts
- Unbilled completed work (`unbilledCents`)
- Avg days close → invoice (`avgDaysCloseToInvoice`)

The last two render in the warning colour above threshold — **$25,000** and **7 days**
respectively. `avgDaysCloseToInvoice` of `null` renders as `—`, never as `0`.

**3 — Snow book.** A two-segment meter from `snowBook`. Label it honestly:

> *N snow contracts · M expire before the season opens*

**Not** "signed / unsigned" — that state does not exist in the database. Omit the whole section when
`activeSnowContracts` is 0.

**4 — Contracts expiring.** The first three of `renewals`, each a name, service type and day count.
Days show in the destructive colour at 30 or fewer.

**5 — Crews today.** From `crewsToday`: crew name, stop count, and the flagged count in the warning
colour when non-zero. Omit the section when the array is empty.

**6 — A `Top customers →` link** at the foot of the rail, routing to `/dashboard/reports`. See the
removals below — this is how that widget's content stays reachable rather than silently vanishing.
