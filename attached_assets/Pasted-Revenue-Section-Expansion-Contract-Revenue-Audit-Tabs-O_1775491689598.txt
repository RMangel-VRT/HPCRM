Revenue Section Expansion — Contract Revenue Audit Tabs
Overview Scope Paragraph

Expand the existing Revenue section of the High Plains CRM into a tabbed revenue workspace instead of creating a separate standalone page. The goal is to preserve the current revenue tracking function while adding a much stronger contract validation and audit workflow. The existing spreadsheet-style revenue matrix should remain available as one tab for broad month-by-month visibility, but a new primary Contract Audit tab should be added to help admin users verify that every customer contract and service line is correctly populated for the selected year. This tab should be built as an operational audit dashboard, not just a prettier spreadsheet. It should surface missing months, inconsistent revenue values, duplicate service lines, zero-value entries, and annual total mismatches in a way that is fast to review and easy to correct. The overall design should feel like one cohesive revenue module with shared filters, shared year selection, and shared data sources across all tabs.

Slice 1 — Convert Revenue Page Into Tabbed Workspace
Objective

Refactor the existing Revenue section so it supports multiple tabs within the same module, while preserving the current revenue matrix view as the starting point.

Why

Right now the revenue section is trying to carry too much on one screen. Before adding audit features, the structure needs to support multiple revenue-specific views in a clean and scalable way.

Revenue Matrix
Contract Audit
Revenue Exceptions
