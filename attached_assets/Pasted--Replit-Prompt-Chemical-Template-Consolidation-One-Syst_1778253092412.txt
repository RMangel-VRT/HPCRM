# Replit Prompt — Chemical Template Consolidation: One System

## Context

The system currently has **two parallel template systems** for chemical-campaign emails, and they have drifted apart in both content and quality. We are consolidating onto the newer one — the **Notification Templates** system at `/dashboard/settings/notification-templates`, backed by the `chemical_notification_templates` table and the `ChemicalNotificationTemplates.tsx` page — and tearing the older one out. After this slice, there is exactly one place an admin edits chemical-campaign emails, exactly one schema that backs them, and exactly one render path that fires when a chemical campaign sends mail.

This slice does not touch non-chemical templates. The Settings → **Email Templates** tab continues to exist for `ticket.work_completed`, welcome emails, and other non-chemical event-rule templates. We are only removing the *chemical* surface area from System 1.

This slice should land in a deployable, production-quality state. Do not stub anything you say you've built. The two original template designs (200+ line polished HTML with greeting, attachment block, applicator card, post-application care list, completion checkmark banner, photos grid, etc.) are sitting in the project root as `chemical-treatment-notification.html` and `chemical-treatment-completion.html` — those are the design source of truth and must be reflected in the consolidated System 2 templates.

## The two systems we are merging

### System 1 — to be removed (chemical entries only)

- **DB:** rows in `email_templates` named `"Chemical Treatment Notice"` and `"Chemical Treatment Completion"`, plus their `email_rules` rows for events `campaign.chemical_pre_notice` and `campaign.chemical_post_notice`.
- **Disk:** `artifacts/api-server/src/templates/chemical-treatment-notification.html` (96 lines, degraded) and `chemical-treatment-completion.html` (112 lines, degraded).
- **Seeder:** `seedChemicalEmailTemplates()` and `CHEMICAL_TEMPLATE_REGISTRY` in `artifacts/api-server/src/templates/seed.ts`.
- **Service exports:** `getDefaultChemicalPreNoticeTemplate`, `getDefaultChemicalPostNoticeTemplate`, `getDefaultChemicalTreatmentNotificationTemplate`, `renderChemicalEmail`, and the `loadTemplateFile()` calls that read those two HTML files in `artifacts/api-server/src/services/emailService.ts`.
- **Render path:** the three `processEmailEvent('campaign.chemical_pre_notice' | 'campaign.chemical_post_notice' | 'campaign.chemical_notification', …)` callsites in `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/routes.ts` (lines 13288, 13341, 13437, 13828, 16041 — confirm with grep before editing).

### System 2 — to remain as the single source of truth

- **DB:** `chemical_notification_templates` table. Columns include `name`, `serviceType`, `isDefault`, `preVisitSubject`, `preVisitHtml`, `postVisitSubject`, `postVisitHtml`, `defaultLabelPdfStorageKey`, `defaultLabelPdfFilename`. One row per template, per company.
- **UI:** `artifacts/highplains-crm/src/pages/ChemicalNotificationTemplates.tsx`, route `/dashboard/settings/notification-templates`.
- **API:** `/api/chemical-notification-templates` CRUD, `/api/chemical-notification-templates/:id/preview`.
- **Seeder:** `seedChemicalNotificationTemplates()` + `CHEM_NOTIF_TEMPLATE_SEEDS` (currently 4 service types) in `artifacts/api-server/src/templates/seed.ts`.
- **Storage:** `getChemicalNotificationTemplate`, `getChemicalNotificationTemplates`, `createChemicalNotificationTemplate`, `updateChemicalNotificationTemplate`, `deleteChemicalNotificationTemplate` in `artifacts/api-server/src/storage.ts` (~lines 4744–4795).
- **Campaign linkage:** `campaigns.notificationTemplateId` already exists and is populated by the campaign-create flow (`routes.ts` line ~12308). The campaign-detail send flows (`routes.ts` lines 12310, 12439, 12552) already resolve directly through this column for chemical campaigns.

The plumbing is mostly there — what's missing is (a) the polished content, (b) full service-type coverage in the seeder, (c) the post-visit-sync bug fix, (d) the cutover of the three `processEmailEvent('campaign.chemical_*')` paths to System 2, and (e) the cleanup of System 1 chemical entries.
