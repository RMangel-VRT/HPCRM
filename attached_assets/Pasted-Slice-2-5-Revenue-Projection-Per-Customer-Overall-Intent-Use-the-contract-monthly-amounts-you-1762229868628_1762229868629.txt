Slice 2.5 — Revenue Projection (Per Customer & Overall)

Intent
Use the contract monthly amounts you’re entering to give a clear picture of:
	•	Projected revenue per customer
	•	Projected revenue per month
	•	Projected revenue for the full year

This is based on the contract values only — no invoices or payments yet.

⸻

A) Customer-Level Revenue View

On each Customer, add a Revenue tab.

That tab should show:
	1.	Annual Projection
	•	One big number: total projected revenue for the selected year for that customer.
	•	This is the sum of all contracts’ monthly amounts for that customer, for that year, respecting contract date ranges and status.
	2.	By Month (Jan–Dec)
	•	A simple layout showing 12 months in a row (or a table) with:
	•	Total projected revenue for each month across all that customer’s contracts.
	•	Optionally, under each month, a small breakdown by service type (Maintenance, Chemical, Snow, etc.) if you want.
	3.	By Contract
	•	A table listing all contracts for that customer with:
	•	Service type
	•	Status (active / paused / ended)
	•	Date range
	•	Annual total for that contract
	•	This lets you see which contract contributes what to the annual projection.
	4.	Year Selector
	•	Ability to switch the year (e.g., 2025, 2026) so you’re not locked to one year forever.

⸻

B) Global “Revenue Overview” Page

Add a Revenue Overview page that summarizes all customers together.

This page should show:
	1.	Month Selector
	•	Example: “Viewing May 2025”
	•	When the selected month changes, all numbers below update.
	2.	Key Totals
	•	Selected Month Total – projected revenue across all customers for the chosen month.
	•	Year-to-Date Total – sum of monthly projected revenue from January through the selected month.
	3.	Table of Customers
	•	One row per customer with at least:
	•	Customer name
	•	Projected revenue for the selected month
	•	Optional: that customer’s annual projection
	•	A link to open that customer’s Revenue tab

This lets you see at a glance which customers are driving the month and the year.

⸻

C) Calculation Rules
	•	Revenue is based entirely on the 12 monthly amounts you set on each contract.
	•	A contract contributes to a month only if:
	•	The month falls within the contract’s start and end dates, and
	•	The contract is not paused.
	•	If a contract is paused, treat all its months as zero while it is paused.
	•	For now, it’s enough that a paused contract is simply excluded from projections.
	•	Ended contracts:
	•	Contribute up to their end date; after that, zero.
	•	Multiple contracts under the same customer:
	•	Their monthly amounts simply add together.

No taxes, no discounts, no multi-year spreading beyond the months you’ve already defined.

⸻

D) Permissions
	•	Everyone (Admin, Office, Ops, Viewer) can view revenue projections.
	•	Only users who can edit contracts (Admin/Office) can change the underlying numbers; the Revenue views themselves are read-only.

⸻

E) Acceptance Criteria

Customer Revenue tab
	1.	For a given customer and year:
	•	The Annual Projection equals the sum of all monthly amounts from that customer’s contracts for that year, respecting date ranges and paused/ended statuses.
	2.	The By Month section shows correct totals:
	•	For each month, the amount equals the sum of all unpaused, in-range contracts’ amounts for that month.
	3.	The By Contract table:
	•	Each contract’s annual total equals the sum of its 12 monthly amounts.
	•	Totals in the table add up to the same Annual Projection shown at the top.
	4.	Changing a contract’s monthly amounts:
	•	Immediately changes the month breakdown and annual total after saving.
	5.	Pausing a contract:
	•	Removes its contribution from both the customer’s Revenue tab and the global overview, until it’s unpaused.

Global Revenue Overview
	6.	Selecting a month:
	•	The Selected Month Total matches the total of all customers’ values for that month.
	•	The Year-to-Date Total matches the sum of monthly totals from January through that month.
	7.	The customer table:
	•	Each row’s value for the selected month matches what you see on that customer’s Revenue tab.
	•	Clicking a customer row opens that customer’s Revenue tab for the same year.

⸻

F) Test Checklist (for you to walk through)

1. Single customer sanity check
	•	Take one test customer:
	•	Contract A: Maintenance with a flat monthly amount all year.
	•	Contract B: Chemical with amounts only in certain months.
	•	Check:
	•	Contract A’s annual total = 12 × its monthly value.
	•	Contract B’s annual total = sum of its chemical months.
	•	Customer Annual Projection = A + B.
	•	Monthly breakdown matches the pattern you see in the contracts.

2. Multi-customer sanity check
	•	Set up at least three customers, each with some contract revenue in a specific month.
	•	Open Revenue Overview:
	•	Choose that month.
	•	Confirm that the Selected Month Total equals the sum of all three customer rows.
	•	Click into a customer and confirm the customer view matches the global view.

3. Status & date range
	•	Create a contract that ends mid-year:
	•	Verify that months after the end date show no contribution.
	•	Mark a contract as Paused:
	•	Confirm its amounts disappear from both the customer view and the global overview.

4. Editing impact
	•	Change one month in a contract:
	•	Customer’s monthly figure and annual figure update.
	•	Global overview’s selected month and YTD totals update too.
