For one customer, open its detail page and confirm you can see:

General info (name, address, main contact).

Tabs/sections for:

Contracts

Rate Sheet

Services Included (by contract)

Revenue (projection)

2. Contracts (per Customer)
2.1 Contract CRUD and listing

Contract A: Maintenance

Contract B: Chemical

Start date, end date, status (Active).

Confirm you get a clear validation message and it does not save.

Create a contract that runs only part of the year (e.g., Apr–Oct) and confirm:

3. Signed Contract PDFs
3.1 Upload, view, download

On a contract:

Upload a valid PDF as the signed agreement.

Verify:

You see the filename, who uploaded it, and when.

You can click to view and download the file.

3.2 File rules & versioning

Try uploading a non-PDF (e.g., a Word file):

Confirm you see a clear error and it’s not accepted.

Upload a replacement PDF:

Confirm:

The new one becomes the “current” file.

The old one is still available in some simple version history.

As a non-admin (e.g., Ops or Viewer):

Confirm you can view/download the PDF but cannot replace or delete it.

Confirm all rates persist and display as currency.

Confirm it’s rejected or corrected with a clear message.

The global revenue overview.

8. Permissions & Roles (End-to-End)

Using your four roles (Admin, Office, Ops, Viewer):

Admin

Can do everything: create/edit customers, contracts, rate sheets, services, monthly amounts, PDFs.

Can access Settings.

Office

Can:

Manage customers and contracts.

Edit monthly amounts.

Edit rate sheets.

Edit services included and their plan.

Cannot access admin-only Settings (if you’ve separated that).

Ops

Can:

View customers, contracts, rate sheets, service plans, revenue views.

View signed contracts.

Cannot:

Edit contracts.

Edit rate sheets.

Edit service plans.

Upload/replace PDFs.

Viewer

Read-only for everything.

No edit/save anywhere.

Confirm that:

Role-based menus match what they’re allowed to do.

Direct access (e.g., via changing URLs) to forbidden actions is blocked with a clear message.
