# Slice V4 — Ticket cards: customer eyebrow on top, badges below the title

**Layout only.** No colour changes, no logic changes, no data changes. Every hue, pill,
badge, testid and conditional stays exactly as Slice V3 left it — this slice only moves
rows around and adjusts type scale.

## Why

On every ticket card the first thing you read is a row of badges, and the customer — the
thing you actually scan for — is buried on the fourth line in muted grey. Reverse it: the
customer becomes a small eyebrow at the top, the title stays the dominant element, and the
badge row drops to where the customer used to be.

## Target layout — all four card surfaces

```
BEFORE                              AFTER
─────────────────────────────       ─────────────────────────────
[type][overdue][T&M]      →         ASPEN RIDGE HOA    #a3f2b1c9  →
Clubhouse bed renovation            Clubhouse bed renovation
  #a3f2b1c9                         [▉ Estimate Request][Overdue][T&M]
[invoice category]                  ─────────────────────────────
📍 Aspen Ridge HOA                  ●━●━○━○  Ready for Billing  Due
─────────────────────────────
●━●━○━○  Ready for Billing  Due
```

Four rows, in this order:

1. **Customer eyebrow** — small, muted, uppercase-ish. Ticket `#id` and the chevron sit
   right-aligned on this same row.
2. **Title** — the dominant element, promoted to `font-semibold`.
3. **Badges** — type badge first, then every other badge that used to be on the old top
   row, plus the invoice-category badge folded in.
4. **Footer** — `border-t` progress / status / due / created. **Completely unchanged.**

### Eyebrow styling (use verbatim on all four surfaces)

```tsx
<div className="flex items-center gap-1.5 min-w-0">
  <MapPin className="w-3 h-3 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
  <span className="truncate text-[11px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.06em] text-muted-foreground">
    {ticket.customer.name}
  </span>
</div>
```

### Title

Change `font-medium` → `font-semibold`. Keep `text-base leading-tight line-clamp-2` and
keep the existing `data-testid={`text-ticket-title-${ticket.id}`}`.

## Step 1 — `components/TicketCard.tsx` (the shared list card)

Inside `<div className="flex-1 min-w-0">` there are currently five sibling blocks. Match
them **by content**, not line number:

* **(A)** `<div className="flex items-center gap-2 flex-wrap">` — opens with
  `<TicketTypeBadge … testId={`text-tickettype-${ticket.id}`} />` and ends with the
  `{!selectionMode && <ChevronRight … ml-auto />}`.
* **(B)** `<div className="flex items-start justify-between gap-2 mt-1">` — the `h3`
  with `text-ticket-title-${ticket.id}` and the `span` with `text-ticket-id-${ticket.id}`.
* **(C)** the invoice-category block, gated on
  `ticket.ticketType?.name === "Invoice" && ticket.invoiceCategory`.
* **(D)** the customer block, gated on `showCustomer && ticket.customer`, currently
  `<div className="flex items-center gap-1 mt-1.5 text-sm text-muted-foreground">` with
  `<MapPin className="w-3.5 h-3.5" />`.
* **(E)** the footer, `<div className="flex items-center flex-wrap justify-between gap-2 mt-3 pt-3 border-t">`.

**Reorder and restructure to:**

```tsx
{/* 1 — customer eyebrow, ticket id, chevron */}
<div className="flex items-center gap-2">
  {showCustomer && ticket.customer && (
    <div className="flex items-center gap-1.5 min-w-0">
      <MapPin className="w-3 h-3 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
      <span className="truncate text-[11px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-[0.06em] text-muted-foreground">
        {ticket.customer.name}
      </span>
    </div>
  )}
  <span
    className="ml-auto shrink-0 font-mono text-xs text-muted-foreground"
    data-testid={`text-ticket-id-${ticket.id}`}
  >
    #{ticket.id.slice(0, 8)}
  </span>
  {!selectionMode && (
    <ChevronRight className="w-5 h-5 shrink-0 text-muted-foreground" />
  )}
</div>

{/* 2 — title */}
<h3
  className="mt-0.5 font-semibold text-base leading-tight line-clamp-2"
  data-testid={`text-ticket-title-${ticket.id}`}
>
  {ticket.title}
</h3>

{/* 3 — badges */}
<div className="flex items-center gap-2 flex-wrap mt-2">
  … the full contents of old block (A), minus the ChevronRight …
  … followed by the invoice-category Badge from old block (C), unwrapped from its
    `<div className="mt-1.5">` but keeping its own conditional and testid …
</div>

{/* 4 — footer, byte-for-byte unchanged */}
```

Rules for this step:

* **`ml-auto` moves off the ChevronRight and onto the `#id` span.** On the old row the
  chevron carried `ml-auto` to push itself right. Now the id + chevron are a right-aligned
  pair, so the *first* of them takes `ml-auto` and the chevron takes plain `shrink-0`.
  Leaving `ml-auto` on both would push the id left and the chevron right with a gap
  between them.
* Row 1 is **not** `flex-wrap`. The eyebrow truncates; it must never push the id onto a
  second line.
* Keep every badge's conditional and `data-testid` exactly as-is:
  `badge-overdue-*`, `badge-worktype-*`, `badge-needs-scheduling-*`,
  `badge-action-type-waiting-*`, `badge-action-type-needs-action-*`,
  `badge-invoice-category-*`, and `text-tickettype-*` on the type badge.
* The `selectionMode` checkbox and the `hue` rail are **outside** `flex-1 min-w-0`. Do not
  touch them.
* Do not change `ticket.ticketType?.name === "Invoice"` — name comparisons are a separate
  slice.
* `MapPin` stays imported; it just gets smaller.

what makes it degrade cleanly rather than wrap or overflow.
