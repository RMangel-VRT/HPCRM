1. Setup & Roles

Task 1.1 – Seed users and roles

Create four users, one in each role: Admin, Office, Ops, Viewer.

Confirm:

All can log in and see the main dashboard.

Only Admin sees the Settings section.

Only Admin/Office see edit actions for customers, contracts, rate sheets, services.

Task 1.2 – Settings sanity

As Admin, open Settings:

Confirm company name, seasons (mowing/cleanup/blowout), benchmarks, and feature flags all load.

Change one benchmark and one flag, save, reload → values stay changed.

As Ops or Viewer, try to access Settings → get a clear “no access” message.

2. Customer Database (CRM Core)

Task 2.1 – Create and manage customers

Create 3 customers with:

Name, address, at least one contact field.

Status: one Active, one Prospect, one Inactive.

Confirm you can:

See them all in a customer list.

Filter by status (e.g., show only Active).

Search by name and find them.

Task 2.2 – Customer details & persistence

Open one customer detail page.

Edit something (e.g., address or contact info), save, reload:

Confirm the change persists.

If Notes are implemented:

Add 2–3 notes, confirm they show with user & timestamp, newest at the top.

3. Contracts per Customer

Task 3.1 – Create multiple contracts under one customer

For a single customer (say “Test HOA 1”):

Create two contracts:

Contract A: Maintenance (full-year dates).

Contract B: Chemical (running only Mar–Oct).

Confirm:

Both appear under that customer’s Contracts section.

Each shows service type, date range, and status.

Task 3.2 – Date & status rules

Try to create or edit a contract where the end date is before the start date:

Confirm it is blocked with a clear validation message.

Change one contract’s status to Paused and another to Ended:

Confirm the status is clearly visible in the contract list.

4. Signed Contract PDF

Task 4.1 – Upload, view, download

On one contract:

Upload a valid PDF as the signed agreement.

Confirm:

The file name, uploader, and upload time are displayed.

There is a button/link to view and download the PDF.

In the contracts list for that customer, this contract shows an indicator like “has signed contract.”

Task 4.2 – Validation & permissions

Try uploading a non-PDF file (e.g., .docx or .jpg):

Confirm you get a clear error, and the file is rejected.

Upload a second PDF to replace the first:

Confirm:

The new one is marked as current.

There is some record of previous versions (e.g., version list or “previous file”).

Log in as Ops or Viewer:

Confirm you can view/download the PDF.

Confirm you cannot replace or delete it (no button, or blocked with a permission error).

5. Monthly Billing Amounts (per Contract)

Task 5.1 – Enter monthly amounts

For one customer:

On the Maintenance contract:

Enter 12 monthly amounts (they can all be equal or seasonal).

On the Chemical contract:

Enter amounts only in the months where the service actually happens.

Save, reload:

Confirm all entered values persist.

Confirm each contract shows an annual total that equals the sum of its months.

Task 5.2 – Validation

Try entering text instead of a number in a monthly field:

Confirm you get a friendly message and cannot save until it is fixed.

Try leaving some months blank (depending on your design):

Confirm the behavior is consistent (either forced to fill or treated as zero, but not random).

6. Customer Rate Sheet (Maintenance, Emergency, Snow & Ice)

Task 6.1 – Enter and persist rates

On a customer’s Rate Sheet:

Fill in realistic values for:

Maintenance: general, operator, irrigation, emergency general, emergency irrigation.

Snow & Ice: hand shovel, plow truck, ATV, skid steer, snow blower, ice melt per pound, ice melt application labor.

Save and reload:

Confirm all rates show correctly as currency.

Confirm any fields you left empty remain empty (not auto-filled).

Task 6.2 – Validation & permissions

Try entering non-numeric and negative values:

Confirm they are rejected or corrected with clear messages.

Confirm:

Admin/Office can edit and save.

Ops/Viewer can see all rates but cannot edit (no Save or blocked).

7. Services Included per Contract (Blueprint)

Task 7.1 – Add services to a Maintenance contract

On the Maintenance contract for a customer:

Go to the Services tab.

Mark as included:

Full mowing service

Pet station service

Shrub trimming

Ornamental grass trimming

Aeration

Cleanups

Tree pruning

Confirm:

Each shows a default annual count and default seasonal pattern (month breakdown).

Mowing defaults to 26 visits with the intended seasonal pattern (2 in April, weekly May–Sep, 2 in Oct).

Task 7.2 – Add services to a Chemical contract

On the Chemical contract:

Include Chemical service and configure:

Organic vs non-organic flag.

Roundup/organic weekly during mowing months.

Broadleaf 4x per year.

Fertilizer apps: 1 spring pre-emergent, 2 summer, 1 fall.

Confirm:

Each sub-component has its own annual count.

The month breakdown reflects the expected pattern (e.g., fertilizer events in the right months).

Task 7.3 – Schedule summary behavior

Open the Schedule Summary inside the Services tab:

Confirm it shows:

One row per included service (e.g., Mowing, Cleanups, Shrub trimming, etc.).

Columns for Jan–Dec with counts in each month.

A total count per service at the end of each row.

Change an annual count (e.g., add more cleanups) or adjust the month distribution:

Confirm the totals and per-month values update correctly.

Save and reload the contract:

Confirm all services, counts, patterns, and notes persist.

Task 7.4 – Permissions

As Ops or Viewer:

Confirm the Services tab and summary are visible.

Confirm no editing is possible (no add/remove, no changes saved).

8. Revenue Projection (Slice 2.5)

Task 8.1 – Customer-level revenue

For a customer with multiple contracts and monthly amounts:

Open the customer’s Revenue tab.

Confirm:

The Annual Projection equals the sum of all contract months for that year.

The Monthly breakdown row (Jan–Dec) matches the sum of that customer’s contracts for each month.

Each contract’s annual total in the “By Contract” list matches its 12-month sum.

Change one contract’s monthly value (e.g., bump up July):

Confirm:

July’s total for that customer changes accordingly.

Annual Projection increases by the same amount.

Task 8.2 – Status/date effects

Mark a contract as Paused:

Confirm its monthly amounts disappear from:

That customer’s Revenue tab.

The overall Revenue Overview.

Change a contract’s end date:

Confirm only months within the new date range count toward revenue.

Task 8.3 – Global revenue overview

Open the global Revenue Overview page.

Select a specific month (e.g., June):

Confirm:

Selected Month Total equals the sum of all customers’ projected June revenue.

Year-to-Date Total equals the sum of January–June projections.

In the customer table:

Each customer’s amount for that month matches what you see on their Revenue tab.

Clicking a customer row opens that customer’s Revenue view for the same year context.

Ask Replit to run one realistic scenario start-to-finish:

Create a new customer representing a real HOA/property you service.

Add:

A Maintenance contract (full year).

A Chemical contract (seasonal months).

Upload the signed PDF for at least one contract.

Enter:

Monthly billing amounts for each contract.

The customer’s full rate sheet.

Services Included with real-world visit counts and month patterns.

Open the Revenue view:

Confirm the projected annual revenue and monthly breakdown make sense intuitively.

Pretend a board member calls and asks:

“What services are included in our contract, how often, and when?”

“What are we paying per month and for the year?”

“Can you send me the signed contract?”

Confirm all three answers can be pulled directly from the system in a few clicks.
