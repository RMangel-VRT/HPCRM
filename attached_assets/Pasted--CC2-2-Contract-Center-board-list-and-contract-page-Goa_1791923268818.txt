# CC2-2: Contract Center board, list and contract page

## Goal

The screens Randy saw in the "High Plains Contract Center" mockup: the renewals board, the all-contracts list and the contract page, with view and edit. They're built on the CC2-1 API. Creating and renewing are CC2-3; this slice leaves the buttons in place and has them open a "Coming in the next update" toast.

Admin and office only. The old Contracts page keeps working at a new address (below).

## Routing and navigation (`App.tsx`, `AppSidebar.tsx`)

- `/dashboard/contracts` → new `ContractCenterPage`.
- `/dashboard/contracts/:id` → new `ContractPage`.
  - If the id isn't an agreement, call `GET /api/contract-center/contracts/by-legacy/:id`.
  - If that finds one, redirect to the agreement (`replace`).
  - Otherwise show "This contract hasn't been copied to the Contract Center yet", with a link to the old page.

  This fixes the revenue audit's broken "Open contract" link once the copy has run.
- The old `ContractsOverview` moves to `/dashboard/contracts/old`. Register it **before** `/dashboard/contracts/:id` so wouter matches it first.
- The sidebar "Contracts" item stays where it is, now pointing at the Contract Center.
- Both new pages use `allowedRoles={["admin", "office"]}`, like the old one.

## Shared bits (`artifacts/highplains-crm/src/components/contracts/`)

- **`LineBadge`:** Landscape (green), Snow (blue), or Landscape + Snow (split). Use the existing theme colors; no new hex values.
- **`StatusPill`:** one colored dot and a label for each `displayStatus`:
  - draft (grey)
  - sent (blue)
  - signed / active (green)
  - expired (amber)
  - renewed (grey)
  - lost (red)
  - cancelled (grey)

  "Up for renewal" is a separate amber pill shown when `renewalDue` is true.
- **`money(cents)`:** `$1,234.56`, or `$1,235` when the cents are zero. Reuse the CC1-4 formatter if it exists.
- **The season label** prints as stored (`2026–27`).

## ContractCenterPage

**Header:** the title "Contracts", a Landscape / Snow toggle (remembered in localStorage, inside try/catch), a search box (customer name), and a **New contract** button.

**Renewals board** (four columns, scrolls sideways on narrow screens). A contract with snow appears on the Snow board; a contract with landscape appears on the Landscape board; a Landscape + Snow contract appears on both.

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Up for renewal** | `renewalDue` and no `renewalDraft`, plus `expired` with no renewal |
| **Renewal drafted / sent** | contracts whose `renewalDraft` is `draft` or `sent` (label on the card says which) |
| **Signed** | terms signed in the last 60 days (`signed_at`), either new contracts or renewals |
| **Lost** | latest term `lost` with `end_date` in the last 12 months |

- **Each card shows:**
  - the customer
  - LineBadge, "Our form" or "Their form", StatusPill
  - season and end date
  - the annual price (or "T&M" / "Flat snow $X")
  - a "Needs review" chip when `needsReview` is true
- **Card buttons:** Renew (Up for renewal), Open (everything else).
- **A progress line above the board:** "<n> of <m> renewals signed", where m = contracts that were due in the last 60 days or are due now. Draw a thin stacked bar.

**All contracts table** (below the board, filtered by the same toggle and search):
- **Columns:** Customer, Type, Form, Season, Status, Annual price, Managing agent, Ends.
- **Sort** by any column; the default is Ends ascending.
- **Filter chips:** Needs review, Up for renewal, Expired.
- **Click a row** to open the contract page.

## ContractPage

**Header:**
- **Text:** the customer name (links to the customer), LineBadge, the form, StatusPill, the managing agent and the project contact.
- **Figures:** Season, Annual price, Monthly (only when the billing type is `equal_monthly`).
- **Buttons:** Edit (current term only), Renew for next season (shown when `renewalDue`, `expired` or `active`; disabled with a tooltip when a renewal draft exists).

**Season timeline:** one chip per term (season, dates, price, status), oldest to newest. Click a chip to view that term. Past terms are read-only, with a "Past season, read only" note. The selected term is in the URL hash (`#term-<id>`).

**Needs review banner:** when the term has `needs_review`, show the `review_note` items as a list in an amber box, with a **Mark reviewed** button. It PATCHes `needsReview: false` and keeps the note.

**Tabs:**

1. **Scope**
   - Grouped by Landscape / Irrigation / Snow, showing only the groups that apply to the line.
   - **Each row:** switch, label, Word section hint (from the catalog's `wordModule`), quantity and unit, and a month-spread mini bar of 12 cells. In edit mode it becomes 12 small number inputs; the sum must equal the quantity, or show an inline warning.
   - **Snow:** a "Snow triggers" text field for the term, defaulting to "Two inches (2”) on sidewalks and steps & Four inches (4”) on streets and drives".
   - **Custom lines:** "Add custom service" adds an `other:<slug>` line with a label.
   - **Save** calls `PUT …/scope`.
2. **Rates**
   - Grouped by line, then category, with columns Rate card, This contract, Unit, Minimum.
   - **Changed rates** (price differs from the card price) get a "Changed" chip and a Reset button in edit mode.
   - **Custom rates** get a "Custom" chip. "Add custom rate" asks for label, line, category, unit, minimum and price.
   - **Save** calls `PUT …/rates`.
3. **Billing**
   - **Each payment line:** its name, yearly total, and a 12-month bar with the amounts. The months on the bars run from the term's start month.
   - **Below:** the expanded schedule table (month, line, amount) for the whole term.
   - **Edit mode** opens the payment editor from CC2-3. Until then, show the lines read-only.
   - **Snow:** shows "Time and materials, invoiced monthly, net 30" or "Flat rate $X, paid on the Snow line".
4. **Documents**
   - The term's files, newest first: kind, version, filename, date, uploaded by. Download goes through `/objects/...`.
   - **Upload buttons:** Signed copy, Their form, Other. They use the CC2-1 upload endpoints and the same upload component as the customer page's contract upload.
   - **After a signed copy is uploaded on a `sent` term**, ask inline "Mark this contract signed?" and, on Yes, call the status endpoint with `to: "signed"`.
5. **History:** status history and edits, newest first (when, who, from → to, note).

**Status actions** (a menu next to the StatusPill): only the moves the CC2-1 API allows for the current status. They're plain buttons; any confirmation is inline. Never use `window.confirm`.

**Edit mode:**
- One Edit button switches every tab of the current term into edit mode; Done saves each changed tab.
- Leaving with unsaved changes shows an inline "Discard changes?" bar.
- For `signed` or `active` terms, show "Changes to a signed contract are recorded in History."

## Tests

`ContractPage` and `ContractCenterPage` render tests (vitest + testing-library, following existing client tests):
- **Board placement:** a Both contract appears on both boards; an expired contract with no renewal appears under Up for renewal; a renewal draft moves its contract to the drafted column.
- **Legacy links:** a legacy id redirects to its agreement.
- **Past terms:** a past term shows no Edit button.
- **Money format:** `$1,500.00` and `$1,175`.
