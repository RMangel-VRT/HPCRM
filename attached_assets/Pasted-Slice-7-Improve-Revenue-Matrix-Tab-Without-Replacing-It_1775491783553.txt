Replit Design Constraint

Use the existing Revenue section as the home for this work and expand it into a tabbed module rather than creating a new standalone page. The UI should feel like a natural extension of the current CRM admin experience, not a separate reporting tool. Keep the current revenue matrix view intact as one tab, then add new tabs for Contract Audit and Revenue Exceptions that use the same shared year selector, filter state, and source revenue data. The Contract Audit tab should become the primary operational validation screen, designed for fast review of contract completeness and revenue population issues. The Revenue Exceptions tab should act as a focused cleanup queue for flagged problems only. All three tabs must stay connected to the same underlying revenue records and should support drilling back to the original source entries rather than introducing duplicate data management patterns.
