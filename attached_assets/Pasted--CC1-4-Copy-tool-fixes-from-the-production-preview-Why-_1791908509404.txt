# CC1-4: Copy tool fixes from the production preview

## Why

Running the CC1-3 preview against production data (Oct 13, 2026: 101 contracts, 50 customers) showed that three copy rules were built on wrong assumptions. Randy confirmed the real rules:

1. **Multi-year contracts are common.** Some run 2–3 years, such as 2024-04-01 to 2026-03-31, or 2025-10-01 to 2028-09-30. The 12-month limit flagged every month on these contracts as "doesn't fit".
2. **The payment schedule is a 12-month cycle that is independent of the service months.**
   - High Plains contracts bill 12 equal payments. Most MSI contracts bill four payments of 5% and eight of 10%.
   - A seasonal contract, for example Apr–Oct, still bills all 12 months.
   - The monthly amounts already hold each contract's real pattern; the copy must place them, not reshape them.
3. **None of the current snow contracts are flat-rate.** Every existing snow contract is time and materials, so monthly amounts on an old snow contract are a data-entry mistake. Flat-rate snow contracts are allowed and will be created in the Contract Center (Phase 2). This rule applies only to copying today's data; don't hard-code snow as T&M anywhere outside the copy tool.
4. **Landscape and snow are one contract only when they share the same signed document.** Matching dates alone don't prove it.
5. **Operator labor** belongs on the rate card. **ATV and snowblower are two separate rates.**

Depends on CC1-1, CC1-2 and CC1-3. No new tables and no migration.

## 2. Copy rules that change (`artifacts/api-server/src/lib/contractCenterCopy.ts`)

Everything else in CC1-3 stays as it is.

### Rule A: billing months are a 12-month cycle

Replace the current placement logic (the `candidates` loop, and the ambiguous-year and "doesn't fit" flags) with this:

1. Ignore amounts whose month number isn't 1–12. If any exist, flag "Monthly amount with invalid month number <n>".
2. `termMonths` = the number of calendar months from the start month to the end month, inclusive (Apr 2024 → Mar 2026 = 24).
3. **If `termMonths >= 12`:** for every calendar month in that range, place that month number's amount on the first of the month. A 24-month contract gets each amount twice; a 16-month contract gets 16 entries.
4. **If `termMonths < 12`:** place the 12 month numbers across the **12 months starting at the start month**, even past the service end. Example: Apr 1 – Oct 31, 2026 bills Apr 2026 through Mar 2027.
5. Skip amounts of 0. No flags for placement.
6. `annual_price_cents` = the sum of the amounts for the 12 month numbers (one year's price), or null if there are none. It is **not** the total over a multi-year term.
7. If `termMonths >= 12` and `termMonths % 12 !== 0`, flag "Contract runs <n> months (not whole years); check the dates".
8. Landscape terms with some, but not all, 12 month numbers entered: flag "Only <n> of 12 monthly amounts entered".

Keep the existing "No monthly amounts entered" flag for landscape terms with none.

### Rule B: billing type comes from the amounts

- **Snow** (snow-only agreements, or the snow side of a merged Both): `time_and_materials` when copying, because no current snow contract is flat-rate.
- **Landscape and Both:**
  - All 12 month numbers present with equal amounts → `equal_monthly`.
  - Any amounts present, but not all equal → `monthly_variable`.
  - No amounts → `equal_monthly`, with the existing "No monthly amounts entered" flag.
- Keep the old billing pattern in `notes` as "Old billing pattern: <x>".

### Rule C: monthly amounts on snow contracts are never billed

- For a snow-only term with amounts: write **no** billing months, set `annual_price_cents` to null, and add each amount to `notes` ("Old snow monthly amount, Nov: $X").
- Flag: "Snow contract had monthly amounts entered, but none of the current snow contracts are flat-rate. Check whether they belong on the landscape contract."
- Delete the old "check whether it's a flat seasonal price" flag.

### Rule D: merge into Both only when the signed document is the same

Group terms as now: by customer, then line, then identical dates. Then, for each customer, compare each landscape term with each snow term.

1. **Two documents are the same** when they have the same `storage_object_path`, or the same `filename` **and** `file_size`.
2. **Same document and identical start and end dates:** merge the two into **one agreement with `line: "both"`** and one term.
   - The term's `legacyContractIds` lists all the old contracts.
   - Scope lines: the union of both. Same-key lines combine as today.
   - Rates: every active card line (`landscape`, `irrigation`, `snow`, `general`).
   - Billing months and annual price: from the landscape contracts only. Snow amounts follow rule C (notes plus the flag).
   - Billing type: from the landscape amounts (rule B).
   - Documents are de-duplicated by the same-document test above. Keep the earliest `created_at` of each.
   - History: both histories, in order.
   - Status: the "more active" of the two (`active` beats `renewed` beats `lost`).
   - `renewed_from` chains are built per agreement after merging.
   - No merge flag on the result.
3. **Same document but different dates:** don't merge. Flag both terms: "Shares a signed document with the <other line> contract but the dates differ (<dates>); check the dates."
4. **Identical dates but no shared document** (different documents, or one or both have none): don't merge. Flag both terms: "Same dates as the <other line> contract but a different or missing signed document; kept separate."
5. **Neither shared document nor identical dates:** don't merge and don't flag. These are genuinely separate contracts, like the 1st Bank sites.
6. Delete the old "Has separate landscape and snow agreements…" flag.

Report types: `CustomerReport.agreements[].line` and the agreement plan's `line` become `"landscape" | "snow" | "both"`.

### Rule E: rate sheet mapping

- Add `rate_operator_labor` ← `operatorLabor` and `rate_snowblower` ← `snowBlower` to the override map.
- `rate_atv` ← `atv` only. Remove the snowblower fallback.
- Delete the two flags "Rate sheet has separate ATV and snowblower rates…" and "Rate sheet has an operator labor rate…".
- If the company's rate card doesn't have `rate_operator_labor` or `rate_snowblower` yet (the missing standard rates were never added), a customer with that rate-sheet value gets the flag "Rate sheet value for <key> skipped; add the missing standard rates in Settings first".

## 3. UI (`LegacyCopyCard.tsx` and `ContractSettings.tsx`)

- Show the line as **Landscape**, **Snow** or **Landscape + Snow**, not the raw key.
- Before a preview has run, the copy button reads **Preview first** and is disabled. Don't show "Copy 0 contracts".
- Group flags by customer in the table, so a customer's landscape and snow rows sit together.
- In the rate card, format money with thousands separators: `$1,500.00`, not `$1500.00`.

## 4. Tests (`contractCenterCopy.test.ts`)

Replace the tests that asserted the removed behavior (ambiguous year, "doesn't fit", the snowblower fallback, the old flags). Add these:

- **Two-year contract:** Apr 1, 2024 – Mar 31, 2026 with 12 equal amounts of 100000 gives 24 billing months, `annual_price_cents = 1200000`, `equal_monthly`, and no flags.
- **Seasonal contract:** Apr 1 – Oct 31, 2026 with 12 amounts gives billing months Apr 2026 through Mar 2027, and the annual price is the full 12-month sum.
- **MSI pattern:** four months at 5% and eight at 10% (for example, 50000 × 4 and 100000 × 8) gives `monthly_variable`, and the annual price is the sum.
- **16-month contract:** Dec 1, 2024 – Mar 31, 2026 gives 16 billing months and the "runs 16 months" flag.
- **Merge:** landscape and snow contracts with identical dates and one document with the same `storage_object_path` become one `both` agreement with one term. The term lists both legacy ids, has one document, rates from all four lines, billing from landscape only, and no merge flag.
- **Same file by name and size:** two document rows with different paths but the same filename and size also merge.
- **Same dates, different documents:** two agreements, each with the "different or missing signed document" flag.
- **Shared document, different dates:** two agreements, each with the "dates differ" flag.
- **Different dates, different documents:** two agreements with no merge-related flags.
- **Snow-only with amounts:** no billing months, a null price, the amounts in notes, and the new snow flag.
- **Rate sheet:** `operatorLabor: 7500` and `snowBlower: 14000` override `rate_operator_labor` and `rate_snowblower`. `atv: 13000` overrides only `rate_atv`.
- **Missing card rows:** a card without `rate_snowblower` and a rate sheet with `snowBlower` set gives the "skipped" flag.
