---
name: Work location ownership
description: The November 19 decisions on editable work location and its document fallback.
---

For an estimate linked to a ticket, the ticket owns the work-location text.
The estimate and proposal screens are editors of that same fact, not owners.
An estimate without a ticket owns its own text. With no owner text, display the
property address. Ticket pins are separate from the printed location.

**Why:** The user wants the location editable from all three screens without
copies drifting or blank customer-facing PDFs.

**How to apply:** Do not backfill or copy location text when linking or deleting
a ticket. Treat location as non-commercial even for sent or frozen estimates,
but never overwrite stored finalized proposal PDFs or issued invoice snapshots.
