Estimate Requests continue as the same ticket into project delivery and never create contracts. RFP Requests produce contracts and never become Projects. Contract renewal may share estimate-builder pricing tools, not estimate lifecycle.

**Why:** The user explicitly distinguishes one-off project opportunities from recurring contract opportunities and prohibits crossover.

**How to apply:** Do not add estimate-to-contract conversion or generalize RFP linking for symmetry. Read the uploaded update before planning either pipeline.

RFP Center belongs inside Contracts as a section-rail page, not in a standalone Bids section. The contract document is the proposal: contract creation happens at Proposal Drafted, not only when won. One RFP may produce multiple contracts; one proposal document represents one contract. Contract lifecycle is authoritative and advances the RFP forward.

**Why:** A property manager must receive the proposed contract for consideration before award; later signature is the award outcome.

**How to apply:** Follow the dedicated Contract Center slice for linking and synchronization. Estimate-only Won work must exclude RFP onboarding and retain final-status and Colorado-day safeguards.
