---
name: Custom catalog-save category boundary
description: Why custom service saves have stricter umbrella rules than general Services Catalog creation.
---

Custom service saves must use the active category's current default umbrella,
while the general Services Catalog still allows per-item umbrella overrides.

**Why:** The category-led Custom dialog was explicitly chosen to determine where
the new line posts. The original brief's “category default or client umbrella”
wording was ambiguous; the approved refinement requires a mismatch error rather
than silently accepting an override. This is deliberately narrower than general
service creation, not a repeal of its override capability.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction when changing either creation flow.
Do not make Custom's derived umbrella editable or relax its server validation
to match the general catalog editor.
