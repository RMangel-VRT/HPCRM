# RV6 handoff — Revenue planning and contract detail

- The existing Revenue route has Income, Planning, Contract detail, and the existing admin-only Setup. Office can read the three planning and two contract-detail views. Planning writes use the initiating company and the existing admin checks.
- The Plan and Renewals views share the RV4 projection. The additional renewal detail uses Contract Center eligibility and lineage. Revenue narrows the at-risk display to 60 Colorado calendar days; it does not change Contract Center's wider action window. The signed drill-down uses each agreement's next signed season after Colorado today, even if that season starts before January. The rail badge counts pending renewals from the next-year plan, which does not have the same date window as the drill-down.
- Target edits send just the changed streams. A monthly edit retains any untouched cent amounts in its stream. Spread allocates whole dollars by largest remainder with calendar-order tie breaking. Nonpositive historical months get no weight; if none are positive the allocation is even. Saving from Plan uses the existing server calculation, not the client grid.
- Monthly billing uses exact stored cents. Year to date is January through the selected billing month. The summary split shows fees separately without changing the existing inclusive contract/snow totals. The searched table and CSV use the same rows. The drawer reuses the agreement detail read and displays its payment schedules; invoiced amounts remain unconnected.
- By customer stays one row per agreement with the agreement owner as the label. Its monthly bars and totals use the visible rows, and renewal markers follow counted agreement history. Glance links to this view for the selected year.
- RV7 can retire only the legacy Revenue components and endpoints identified in the earlier handoff. Keep the new `revenue-v2` components, shared planning routes, and the Revenue contract reads. No migrations or legacy table writes are needed for RV6.
